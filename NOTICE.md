## Modifications made for TMJ Atlas Lab

The source images were modified to create this simulator. Modifications include:

- removal of the exterior white background;
- separation of the fixed cranium and movable mandibular body;
- separation of the lower dental row;
- removal of the source condylar head in the movable layer;
- creation of transparent and monochrome reference layers;
- addition of runtime-rendered temporal bone, articular eminence, mandibular condyle, articular disc, joint spaces, capsule, ligaments, motion paths, labels, and measurement overlays;
- mirroring for left-side observation;
- opacity and focus processing in the browser.
